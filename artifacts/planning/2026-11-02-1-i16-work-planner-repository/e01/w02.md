**Component:** workspace:scripts/update-workspace.sh

## Overview

In m2ux/workflow-server, `update-workspace.sh` leaves a workspace's submodules at the commits it pins once the template merges in, so a workspace that vendors a skill keeps it checked out across template updates.

## Problem

`update-workspace.sh` fetches the template and merges it, and stops there. A submodule the workspace adds or moves stays empty, or at its old commit, in every other checkout of that workspace until someone checks it out by hand.

## Proposal

The scene is a workspace forked from workflow-server's `workspace` template, which declares its own submodules under `vendor/`.

- **Submodules follow the merge.**
  After a clean merge, the update checks out each submodule the workspace declares at its pinned commit.
- **A conflicted merge stops first.**
  A merge that stops on a conflict leaves the submodules alone.
