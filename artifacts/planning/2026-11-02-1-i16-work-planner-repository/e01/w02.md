**Component:** workspace:scripts/update-workspace.sh

## Overview

`update-workspace.sh` leaves a workspace's submodules at the commits the merged template pins, so moving the template's pin moves every workspace's skill at its next update.

## Problem

`update-workspace.sh` fetches the template and merges it, and stops there. A submodule the template adds stays empty, and one whose pin the template moves stays at its old commit.

## Proposal

The scene is a workspace forked from workflow-server's `workspace` template, updated after the template adds or moves a submodule.

- **Submodules follow the merge.**
  After a clean merge, the update checks out each submodule at the commit the merged template pins.
- **A conflicted merge stops first.**
  A merge that stops on a conflict leaves the submodules alone.
